## Recommended actions

1. **Keep the three active sales campaigns unchanged through the 72-hour mark.** Avoid budget, targeting or creative edits while the launch is still stabilising.
2. **Treat Maleny Jute as the leading hypothesis, not a graduated winner.** It needs checkout and purchase evidence before scaling.
3. **Preserve the catalogue campaign as the control.** It is still the cheapest source of clicks and is the only campaign with a checkout in this window.
4. **Confirm whether the Trade campaign should remain paused.** Its lack of delivery is explained by status, not by audience or creative performance. Reactivation would restore the planned A$10/day budget.
5. **Reconcile Shopify orders with Meta purchases after attribution backfill.** If Shopify recorded orders while Meta remains at zero, audit Pixel/CAPI purchase tracking before making creative decisions.
6. **At day 7, review delivery-starved prospecting ads.** With six ads and A$100/day, the seven-day half-fair-share threshold is approximately **A$58 per ad**. Ads still materially below that level can be treated as Meta-deprioritised; today is too early for that call.
