# The Inspire Home — Meta Creative Batch

Prepared for The Inspire Home Australia. This first batch contains ten non-UGC, portrait-first static creatives using product photography from the live website.

## Recommended campaign structure

| Funnel | Ad set | Creatives |
|---|---|---|
| Prospecting | Broad Australia | 01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 07, 09 |
| Prospecting | Home-interior interests | 01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 09 |
| Retargeting | Website visitors, 30 days | 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 08 |
| Retargeting | View content / add to cart, 14 days | 02, 06, 07, 08 |
| Trade | Designers, stylists, architects | 10 |

Keep the same creative mix in Broad and Interests initially. Let Meta reveal which messages work before splitting winners into dedicated ad sets.

## Generation direction

- Format: portrait-first Meta feed static
- Style: warm Australian interiors, premium editorial restraint, soft neutral palette
- Product rule: preserve the photographed rug’s real design, colour, pile and material appearance
- Copy rule: only verified product and store claims; no invented pricing, discounts, reviews or guarantees
- Exclusion: no UGC treatment, testimonial frames or influencer-style layouts

## Launch notes

- Start with 01, 03, 04, 07 and 09 in Broad Australia.
- Use 06, 07 and 08 more heavily for warm audiences.
- Keep 10 isolated in a trade campaign with its own lead or application objective.
- Run each creative long enough to collect meaningful spend before judging it; compare thumb-stop rate, outbound CTR, landing-page views, add-to-cart rate and purchase ROAS together.
