# The Inspire Home Brand Guidelines

## Brand foundation

The Inspire Home is a New South Wales rug brand for considered Australian interiors. The brand treats a rug as the foundation of a room: practical enough for everyday homes, but selected for texture, character and lasting visual appeal.

**Positioning:** thoughtfully chosen rugs that combine timeless design, material character and practical support for Australian homes.

**Personality:** calm, warm, design-literate, quietly confident and helpful. Never loud, disposable, trend-chasing or aggressively promotional.

## Voice and messaging

Write in concise Australian English. Lead with the room or emotional outcome, then support it with a verified product fact.

Preferred language includes: *considered*, *grounded*, *natural texture*, *warmth*, *character*, *crafted*, *quiet impact*, *made for Australian living* and *designed for real homes*.

Avoid generic luxury claims, breathless superlatives, fake urgency, invented testimonials and words such as *cheap*, *must-have* or *best ever*. Do not call every product sustainable, fair-trade or hand-knotted; those claims require product-level evidence.

## Visual identity

The site uses a restrained editorial system with warm neutrals, generous whitespace, fine rules and a contrast between light grotesk and expressive serif typography.

### Core palette

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm ivory | `#F9F6F2` | Primary canvas |
| Ink | `#121212` | Headlines, body and dark buttons |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean canvas and reversed type |
| Olive taupe | `#6E6855` | Rules, subdued copy and natural accents |
| Sand | `#D8C8AD` | Soft panels and material-led accents |
| Terracotta | `#774538` | Select warm accents |
| Deep navy | `#27394C` | Select high-contrast accents |
| Muted gold | `#AF8E57` | Fine highlights only |

The ivory/ink/taupe combination is the default. Accent colours should follow the rug being advertised, rather than forcing every product into beige.

### Typography

- Website headings: Tomato Grotesk ExtraLight/Light.
- Website body: Segma Regular.
- Editorial accent: Juana ExtraLight/Light.
- Legacy theme fallbacks found on the site: Lora and Tenor Sans.

Use uploaded brand fonts when licensed and available. If Canva cannot access them, document the selected fallback; do not silently mix unrelated type styles.

### Photography and composition

- Preserve the real rug pattern, colour, pile, edge and material appearance.
- Use warm natural light and believable Australian residential spaces.
- Make the rug the dominant product, not background decoration.
- Mix full-room context, material close-ups and clean product-led layouts.
- Keep overlays restrained and readable on mobile.
- Avoid artificial architecture, distorted furniture, impossible rug geometry and stock-photo gloss.

## Creative angle system

Every batch should deliberately span different motivations:

1. **Room transformation** — the rug as the foundation of a finished room.
2. **Material and craft** — wool, jute, texture or construction, verified per product.
3. **Colour and character** — rich green, rust, brown and multicolour designs as well as neutrals.
4. **Practical confidence** — size choice, style quiz, AR visualiser and styling support.
5. **Trust and service** — free standard shipping in Australia and conditional 30-day returns.
6. **Product and price** — current Shopify price, size availability and a direct product CTA.
7. **Offer** — approved discounts only, with terms and dates supplied by the owner.

## Claims and safeguards

Globally verified claims:

- Free standard shipping on all orders within Australia.
- Change-of-mind returns accepted within 30 days of delivery when policy conditions are met.
- NSW-based team serving Australian homes.
- Style quiz, AR visualiser and styling support are available.

Product material, construction, price, inventory and availability must come from the live Shopify Admin API. Discount claims must be explicitly approved. Customer quotes may only come from stored review or comment records with a source reference.

The website currently contains broad sustainability, fair-trade and hand-knotted statements. Treat them as restricted until evidence confirms which products they cover.
