# The Inspire Home Product Marketing Context

## Product and audience

The Inspire Home sells rugs for Australian homes across contemporary, classic, transitional, junior, wool and jute collections. The core buyer is design-conscious and wants a room to feel resolved without sacrificing practicality. Secondary audiences include families choosing kids' rugs and Australian designers or commercial partners.

## Positioning

The brand offers thoughtfully selected rugs with material character, timeless design and practical shopping support. The strongest message is that the right rug becomes the foundation of a warm, considered room.

Primary differentiators:

- Broad material, colour and size choices.
- Free standard shipping within Australia.
- Conditional 30-day returns.
- Style quiz, AR visualiser and access to styling support.
- Australian/NSW context and a restrained editorial aesthetic.

## Voice and creative rules

Use calm, concise Australian English. Prefer grounded sensory language over hype. Preserve the real product in every generated image. Never invent price, inventory, reviews, certifications, discounts or product construction.

The canonical visual, voice and claims specification is [the brand guideline](../brand/the-inspire-home/brand-guidelines.md). Machine-readable tokens live in [brand-tokens.json](../brand/the-inspire-home/brand-tokens.json).

## Evidence and source priority

Use sources in this order:

1. Shopify Admin API for current product, price, inventory and paid-order evidence.
2. Approved offer configuration for discounts and dates.
3. Website policies and brand documents for global claims.
4. Meta reports for creative performance.
5. Stored customer reviews and comments for verbatim customer language.
6. DataForSEO for external search and competitor signals.

Models can interpret these sources but cannot create facts. The public reviews page did not expose usable review text during the 16 August 2026 crawl, so testimonial creative remains blocked until sourced reviews or comments are added.
